Part A: Existing Technology (Pen Scanners)

Did you know that in the business world they use pen scanners?

  • Pen scanners are portable
  • They are used by administrative professionals
  • They quickly digitize physical documents
  • Examples include resumes, business cards, and handwritten notes
  • The scanned text can be converted into searchable and editable files
  • Pen scanners can translate different languages
  • They can also read words out loud
  • They were originally made for people with dyslexia and other disabilities
  • Some pen scanners can scan barcodes
  • They have a large vocabulary
  • Sometimes the volume is too low and requires headphones
  • The battery can run out quickly
  • They are expensive ($50–$250)
